About My Spelling Game

My Spelling Game is a spelling practice tool for students, parents, teachers, and homeschool families. Students can practice custom word lists without creating accounts. Parents and teachers can sign in to a workspace to save lists, publish assignments, and track progress. Parent and Teacher Plans add Smart Review, Today’s Review, photo import, and automatic example sentences.

Mission

My Spelling Game helps students practice the exact spelling words they already receive from school, home, tutoring, or homeschool lessons. Practice stays fast: paste words, practice, and review mistakes. For repeated use, parents and teachers can save lists, assign practice, and follow progress across sessions.

Why It Exists

Many spelling products use their own fixed vocabulary, while families usually need this week's exact words. One practice can be completed completely anonymously. When people need longer-term use, the workspace adds saved lists, assignments, and student progress without changing the custom-list workflow. Parent and Teacher Plans add Smart Review and Today's Review.

What It Does

  • Practice your own spelling words in Spelling Test or Typing Rain.
  • Add optional example sentences for dictation practice.
  • Replay missed words immediately after a practice round.
  • Save and reuse weekly spelling lists in a workspace.
  • Import school lists from a photo and edit the detected words before use.
  • Fill example sentences from the curated library and use Today’s Review on paid plans.
  • Publish assignments without requiring student accounts.
  • Track student progress across assignments.
  • Identify missed words that need later review.
  • Use cross-day practice history to distinguish learning from mastered words.

About the Developer

My Spelling Game is maintained by an indie developer. The product focuses on making weekly spelling practice easy to start, easy to repeat, and easier for adults to follow over time.

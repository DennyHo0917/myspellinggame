Privacy Policy

Data Collection

My Spelling Game is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data:

Local Storage

Your spelling list and optional hear-words preference are stored locally in your browser using localStorage.

Photo import reads the image in your browser; it is not uploaded to our server, and you can edit detected words before using them.

Assignments published by a teacher or parent and student results are stored in Cloudflare D1 for 14 days on Free Plan or 365 days on Parent and Teacher Plans. Student email, IP address, and User-Agent are not collected.

New share links store the word list in the URL fragment, which is processed by your browser and not sent to our server.

You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

Analytics

Google Analytics receives a cleaned page address without query parameters or URL fragments.

Events contain aggregate details such as mode, word count, and result.

We do not send your word list or typed answers to Analytics.

You can opt out by using browser extensions or privacy settings.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display relevant advertisements.

Ad personalization follows Google's privacy policies.

You can manage ad preferences through Google's Ad Settings.

Your Rights

You can clear your saved spelling list at any time.

You can disable analytics through browser settings.

You can block advertisements using ad blockers.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through our website.

Three Ways to Practice Your Own Words

Spelling Test reads one hidden word at a time and grades the typed answer. Typing Rain keeps the falling-word challenge. Typing Chase turns sentence-typing speed into a chase to catch the thief.

Why A Custom Spelling List Matters

A pasted list keeps practice focused on the words needed now. The three modes offer different ways to practice without a student account; Spelling Test and Typing Rain also collect missed words for a focused retry round.

  • Use your own custom word list: Paste words from a teacher handout, homework sheet, grade-level list, or homeschool curriculum.
  • Run a real audio test: The spelling stays hidden until the answer is submitted.
  • Use context without giving away the answer: In an example sentence, the target word appears as a blank while audio reads the word and the full sentence.
  • Use missed-word replay: Incorrect or missed words are saved for another round.
  • Build sentence-typing speed: Typing Chase measures speed and accuracy while the player tries to catch a thief.
  • Share a practice link: Copy the same list and selected mode without an account.
  • Use paid workspace tools: Import a school list photo, fill curated example sentences, and review words due today.

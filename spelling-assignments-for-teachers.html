Quick FAQ

Do students need accounts? No. Students can open an assignment link directly or join a class with a code and personal PIN.

Can I use my own spelling list? Yes. Paste the exact words your class is learning, then create and share the assignment.

What results can teachers track? Workspace records submissions, scores, missed words, progress, and mastery. Teacher Plan also includes CSV export and longer history.
